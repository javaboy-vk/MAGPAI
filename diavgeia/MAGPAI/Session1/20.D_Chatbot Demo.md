# Demo 20.D - Chatbot Demo


## Say This

Now we can put the pieces back together in a chatbot interface.

The question is:

```text
are mag sales up in Chicago?
```

The chatbot does not begin with a final answer.

It follows the pipeline we have been building:

```text
text -> tokens -> vectors -> tiny model -> data lookup -> chart -> response
```

The trace panel is important because it makes the hidden steps visible.

It shows the tokens, the vectors, the model output, and the sales data used to produce the answer.

## Demo Action

Open the Chatbot Demo.

Type or paste:

```text
are mag sales up in Chicago?
```

Click `Run`.

Then enable `Trace`.

Point out:

- The tokens extracted from the question.
- The vectors used by the tiny model.
- The model output.
- The sales data.
- The generated chart.

Then type an unsupported question to show the boundary of the v0.1 teaching chatbot.

## Transition

The chatbot answers directly.

The next demo adds agentic behavior: a goal, a plan, tools, actions, observations, reasoning, and a final answer.



## Key Points

- The chatbot is the user-facing version of the pipeline.
- The trace exposes the hidden model steps.
- The answer is backed by demo sales data.
- The chart is generated from the data.
- The v0.1 chatbot has intentionally narrow supported behavior.
