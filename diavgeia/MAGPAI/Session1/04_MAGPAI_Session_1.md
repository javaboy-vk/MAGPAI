# Slide 04 - MAGPAI Session 1

## Say This

This session is about how a business question becomes numbers, structure, and then a chart-backed answer.

When we ask a question, we think in business meaning. But an AI model does not start there. A neural network needs numbers before it can compute anything.

So the core idea for this session is:

```text
AI models do not read words directly.
They process numeric patterns.
```

Everything in this demo is a small, visible version of that idea.

Our canonical question is:

```text
Are MAG sales up in Chicago?
```

How can possibly a machine know what MAG stands for?
Or what sales means?
Or what Chicago means of whether it is a geographic location?
