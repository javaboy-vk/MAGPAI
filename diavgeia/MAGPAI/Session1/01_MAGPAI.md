# Slide 01 - MAGPAI

## Slide Intent

Open the MAGPAI demo series and make the topic feel inspectable.

## Say This

Today I am starting the MAGPAI AI-under-the-covers demo series.

The purpose is simple: I want to make AI less mysterious by showing the actual transformations that happen inside a language system.

For this first session, we are starting at the very beginning:

```text
Question -> Tokens -> Vectors -> Data Request -> Chart-Backed Answer
```

The point is not to build a production model today. The point is to make the mechanics visible.

## Key Points

- MAGPAI is a learning and demo lab.
- AI is not magic.
- We can inspect the steps.
- Session 1 starts with a business question becoming numbers.
- The final answer comes from data and tools, not model memory.
