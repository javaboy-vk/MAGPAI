# Slide 01 - MAGPAI

## Slide Intent

Open the MAGPAI demo series and make the topic feel inspectable.

## Say This

This is the MAGPAI AI-under-the-hood presentation and demos.

The purpose is simple: I want to make AI less mysterious by showing the actual transformations that happen inside a language system.

Magpie is the name of this beautiful bird.
With a creative spelling MAGPAI will be the name of my Practical AI model, demos and agents.

For session we are starting at the very beginning:

```text
Question -> Tokens -> Vectors -> Data Request -> Chart-Backed Answer
```

The point is not to build a production model today. The point is to make the mechanics visible. I wrote MAGPAI so I will have a point of reference, a tool to help us visualize the underlying concepts.

This presentation is not about Kiro, Bedrock, Amazon Q, or how to use any LLM like ChatGPT.
I want to show how AI understands a picture of a bird, or what the word Sales means. 
I will try to show what training the model, and how the model "thinks".


## Transition

I want first to define our starting point in the larger AI/ML stack.

If we had to define AI as a stack of layers, where each one was based and build upon the previous one, then we can have a mental model of technologies.



## Key Points

- MAGPAI is a learning and demo lab.
- AI is not magic.
- We can inspect the steps.
- Session 1 starts with a business question becoming numbers.
- The final answer comes from data and tools, not model memory.
